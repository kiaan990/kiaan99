    Every building is a real part of kiaan-cloud. Lit windows mean a service is running. Smoke and a red marker mean it stopped. The forge fire follows CPU, the water tower fills with memory, the barrel with swap, and the warehouse crates with disk space. Clouds move faster under load, and a storm rolls in when CPU or memory runs hot. Sky and clock follow New York time. Quests and events you add, finish or delete here change your real task list and iCloud calendar. Trees, lanterns, villagers and XP are just for fun and stay in this browser only.
